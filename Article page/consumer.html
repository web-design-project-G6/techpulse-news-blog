Casio F-B100W Smartwatch

The Casio F-B100W is a modern version of the classic F-91W digital watch. It keeps the retro design while adding Bluetooth and a step counter.

Smart Features

Bluetooth, step counter, phone finder, and automatic time correction.

The US price is $64.95. It comes in black with blue highlights, all black, and green.
