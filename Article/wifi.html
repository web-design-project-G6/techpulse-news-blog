Wi-Fi 8 Routers: What You Need to Know

Wi-Fi 8 is the next generation of wireless networking, designed for faster and more reliable connections.

By Dara Tech Oct 1, 2026 ◷ 3 min read

Wi-Fi 8 Routers

Wi-Fi 8 is a new generation of wireless networking. It is designed to provide faster and more reliable connections when many devices are connected.

TP-Link Archer 8 Ultra

TP-Link has opened preorders for its Archer 8 Ultra, a Wi-Fi 8 router with speeds of up to 19 Gbps.

Speed

Up to 19 Gbps

Antennas

18 antennas

Where Is It Available?

The router is available in selected regions, including Canada, the UK, Australia, and Germany.

Why Is It Restricted in the US?

TP-Link cannot currently sell this new router in the US because of an FCC restriction affecting newly approved foreign-made consumer routers.

Important

Existing approved TP-Link routers can still be used and sold in the US. The restriction mainly affects new products.

Why It Matters

Wi-Fi 8 could improve internet performance in homes, schools, and offices with many connected devices.

More Devices

Better support for phones, computers, cameras, and smart devices.

Better Connections

Designed for faster and more reliable networking.

Key Takeaway

Wi-Fi 8 brings new networking features, but availability depends on regional regulations. The Archer 8 Ultra may need US FCC approval or an exemption before it can be sold in the United States.
